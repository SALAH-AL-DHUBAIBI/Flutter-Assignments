# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-01-17

This "Safe Edition" of Smart Call & SMS Manager respects your privacy by design.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

## 2. No Sensitive Permissions
- **No Call Log Access**: We cannot read your past calls.
- **No SMS Access**: We cannot read your messages.
- **No Phone State Monitoring**: We do not know when you are on a call.

## 3. Local Storage
We only store a log of actions **you explicitly initiate** within the app (e.g., clicking "Launch Dialer"). This data is stored in a local SQLite database and can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

## 4. Notifications
We use the `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` permission solely to deliver the reminders you schedule.

## 5. Contact
If you have questions, please contact the developer.
